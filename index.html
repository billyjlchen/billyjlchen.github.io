SEC.01 // ABOUT

About Me

Two things shaped my path into engineering: seeing the Milky Way for the first time as a kid and realizing how little of the universe we've actually explored, and watching Mark Rober videos that sparked my love for robotics and space exploration.

Now I'm an Engineering Physics student at the University of British Columbia, finally building the math and practical skills to match that spark. On the UBC Rover team, I spend most of my time in the shop or in CAD, from designing resin-casted wheels and prototyping rocker-bogie suspension adapters, to wiring up avionics for deployable relay modules. I like experimenting and working on hardware that solves real world problems. My long-term goal is to work on robotic systems and exploration vehicles that help push humanity a little further into space.
