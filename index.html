SEC.01 // ABOUT

I got into robotics the way most people do — by taking something apart and being disappointed I couldn't put it back together. Space hardware is what kept me hooked: machines that have to work the first time, with nobody around to fix them if they don't.

Most of my time now goes into SolidWorks or standing over a resin mold, chasing the gap between what the CAD says and what the physical part actually does. Wheels, suspensions, deployable payloads, robot legs — if it has to move reliably somewhere hostile, I want to be the one building it.
